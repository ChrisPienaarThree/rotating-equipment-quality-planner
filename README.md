# Rotating Equipment Quality Planner

Build ITP-style quality plans for rotating equipment from a maintenance and inspection requirement register:
standards, maintenance and inspection tasks, intervals by criticality class, acceptance criteria, hold points,
service overlays (oxygen, sour, hydrogen, slurry, cryogenic) and a frequency schedule, then keep them in Excel.

**Open the app:** https://chrispienaarthree.github.io/rotating-equipment-quality-planner/

- Works in any modern browser. It is laid out for a computer or tablet; on a phone it opens zoomed out (pinch to zoom). Plans are kept in your own browser.
- In Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on a computer you can connect the companion workbook
  (`Rotating Equipment Quality Plans.xlsx`) and every plan is saved into it automatically.
- Other browsers: use Export / Import in the Plan register tab.
- The user guide is in `Rotating Equipment Quality Planner - User Guide.pdf`.

The register values are a **starting point only**: typical industry practice for continuous-duty process plant.
Reconcile them with the site's engineering specifications, OEM manuals, criticality register and regulations
before use. Standards are cited without edition numbers; use the edition adopted by the site.

Course material for *Quality Control of Rotating Equipment* (Istec Training).
